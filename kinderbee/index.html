🎓 Early childhood development pathways

Our Preschool Programmes

Each stage matches how children grow — building curiosity, emotional confidence and early literacy and numeracy step by step.

Ages 1.5 – 2.5 Years

Playgroup (Toddler Explorers)

A soft, happy first step from home into school, built around the senses, movement and bonding.

  • Sand, water and sensory play
  • Gentle settling-in routines
  • Rhymes, sounds and story circles
Enquire for Playgroup
Ages 2.5 – 3.5 Years

Nursery (Curious Discoverers)

Growing talk, early sound awareness, hands-on creativity and everyday independence.

  • Painting, clay and messy art
  • Early phonics, songs and new words
  • Sharing and playing together
Enquire for Nursery
Ages 3.5 – 4.5 Years

Junior KG / LKG (Young Thinkers)

First steps in reading, number sense, sequencing and working with friends.

  • Jolly Phonics and pre-writing skills
  • Counting, sorting and patterns
  • Nature walks and “why” questions
Enquire for Junior KG
Ages 4.5 – 5.5 Years

Senior KG / UKG (Future Achievers)

Getting confident, expressive children ready for Grade 1 through reading, inquiry and problem-solving.

  • Reading on their own and building sentences
  • Early addition, subtraction and logic
  • Smooth NEP 2020 primary transition
Enquire for Senior KG

A safe, caring second home for working families — rest, meals, hobbies and attentive caregivers.

  • Healthy meals and CCTV-monitored nap areas
  • Evening crafts and hobby corners
  • Trained, caring early-years staff

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about admissions, teacher training and partnerships.

1. What is the Kinderbee Integrated Partnership System (KIPS)?

KIPS is Kinderbee's partnership system that gives schools complete academic, operational, branding and teacher-training support.

2. Why choose the Kinderbee Preschool Franchise?

Zero recurring royalty on tuition, a proven Nordic-inspired curriculum, turnkey setup and ongoing teacher and marketing support.

3. What is the process for starting a Kinderbee Preschool?

Book a consultation, finalise your site, and our team handles campus design, kits, staff training and admissions launch with you.

4. Who can become a Kinderbee Preschool Partner?

Edupreneurs, existing school owners and anyone passionate about quality early childhood education.

5. What makes the Kinderbee curriculum distinctive?

It's play-based and Nordic-inspired, focusing on inquiry, hands-on discovery and whole-child development rather than rote learning.

6. What ongoing support does Kinderbee provide to its partners?

Curriculum updates, continuous teacher development, operational guidance and local lead-generation assistance.

7. How does the Kinderbee curriculum align with NEP 2020?

It follows NEP 2020's foundational-stage goals — play-based learning, early literacy and numeracy, and a smooth move into Grade 1.

8. What is FinnishWay Academy?

Kinderbee's teacher-development partner, offering Nordic-inspired early childhood training and certification.

9. Is prior experience in education required to become a Kinderbee partner?

No. Our training and setup support are built to guide first-time edupreneurs at every step.

10. Can I rebrand my existing preschool with Kinderbee?

Yes. Existing preschools can convert to Kinderbee and adopt our curriculum, training and branding.
