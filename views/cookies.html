Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Cookies on this website

The public website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

The only cookie we set is a strictly necessary, secure session cookie for our team when they sign in to the admin portal. Your browser may also store small preferences locally (for example, which roadmap items you have ticked on your report). These are never shared.

Third-party content

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook open those services, which have their own cookie policies.

Changes

If we add analytics in future, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.
