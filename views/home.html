What does the free SEO audit include?

The free audit crawls up to 10 pages of your live website and gives you an overall SEO score with category scores, and checks titles, meta descriptions, headings, image ALT text, broken links, canonical tags, your sitemap and robots.txt, indexability, HTTPS, and speed and mobile indicators — with evidence for every finding.

What do the paid audits add?

The 25-page audit (₹{{price.p25}}) checks 25 pages and adds how to fix every issue, with platform-specific steps. The 50-page SEO Growth Audit (₹{{price.p50}}) checks 50 pages and adds priority, impact and difficulty for every issue, a personalised 4-week roadmap and a downloadable PDF report.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee Google rankings. We show you the evidence, fix what is holding your website back, and report on real results such as visibility, traffic and enquiries.

Can you fix the issues the audit finds?

Yes. Our team handles SEO implementation, website fixes and redesigns, so the same people who find the problems can solve them.

Which services do you offer?

Digital marketing (Meta and Google Ads, social media and branding), SEO, web development, UI/UX design, and CRM and app development.
