Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what Click2Client Media ("we") collects through this website, why, and how you can contact us about it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry and audit forms: your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, business name, website address and any message you send.
  • Audit data: publicly available information from the website you ask us to audit (pages, headers, links, images and similar).
  • Paid audits: your order details and the payment status our team records after verifying your payment. We never ask for or store UPI PINs, OTPs, card numbers, CVVs or banking passwords.
  • Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address, used for security and rate limiting.

How we use it

To run and deliver your SEO audit, reply to your enquiry, verify payments, send your report, and — if you ask — discuss our services. We do not sell your information.
