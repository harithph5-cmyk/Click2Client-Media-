What Baasha can and cannot do

Chat answers from the AI model. Research searches the web and cites the pages it found. Agent plans a task and uses tools: web search, reading public web pages, a calculator, the date and time, and saving a report.

It cannot send messages, spend money, delete, publish or change anything, and it only reads public web pages (never private or internal addresses). Web content is treated as untrusted. Links in agent answers are checked against what the tools really returned.

Your data

Conversations and reports are saved only in this browser. The text you send is processed by Google Gemini to produce answers. Do not share passwords or private identity numbers.
